# Scope and Hoisting Worksheet — Answer Key
1. `var` is function-scoped; `let` and `const` are block-scoped.
2. `var` declarations are hoisted and initialized as `undefined`.
3. `let` and `const` are hoisted but remain in the temporal dead zone until declared.
4. A `const` variable cannot be reassigned; object contents may still be changed.
5. A function declaration can be called before its declaration.
6. A function expression follows the hoisting behavior of its variable declaration.
