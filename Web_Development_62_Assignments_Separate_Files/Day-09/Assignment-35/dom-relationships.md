# DOM Relationships for dom-tree.html
- `document` is the root document object.
- `html` is the document element.
- `body` is a child of `html`.
- `main#app` is a child of `body`.
- `h1` and `section.panel` are children of `main`.
- `h2`, `p.name`, and `ul` are descendants inside `section.panel`.
- Each `li` is a child of `ul`; the two `li` elements are siblings.
